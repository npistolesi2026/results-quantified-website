The RQ method

From “something is wrong” to a measurable intervention.

Most operating problems persist because the information is fragmented, ownership is unclear, or nobody has quantified the cost. RQ turns the problem into a decision.

Discuss a diagnostic →
  1. 01

    Map the truth

    Identify where information lives, which sources are authoritative, what is missing, and where the records disagree.

  2. 02

    Quantify the leakage

    Translate excess inventory, delays, overtime, rework, lost capacity, and management drag into defensible financial impact.

  3. 03

    Rank the opportunity

    Prioritize each issue by impact, confidence, difficulty, ownership, and time to value.

  4. 04

    Build the fix

    Implement the model, workflow, control, or reporting system—and define how the result will be measured.

Questions

What this is—and what it is not.

Is Results Quantified an AI consulting company?

AI is part of the leverage, not the thing being sold. The product is operational clarity, quantified opportunity, better systems, and measurable follow-through.

What information does a diagnostic require?

It depends on the problem, but may include order, inventory, purchasing, labor, production, financial, meeting, task, or management-reporting data. Scope and access are defined before work begins.

Do you replace our ERP, accountant, or internal team?

No. RQ works across the systems and people you already have, identifies the gaps between them, and builds the operating layer needed to make the information useful.

Do you guarantee savings or revenue growth?

No responsible diagnostic can guarantee an outcome before the evidence is reviewed. RQ separates verified findings, estimates, assumptions, and unresolved questions so decisions are based on what the data actually supports.
